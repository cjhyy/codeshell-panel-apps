# Resume layout rubric

Evaluate the actual target state: Panel preview, exported A4/PDF, or ATS upload.

## Blocking failures

- clipped, overlapping, hidden, or unreadable public text;
- essential information stored only in an image, icon, header, or footer;
- broken reading order or unselectable body content in an ATS deliverable;
- internal evidence controls, placeholders, or Panel actions in the export;
- body text below a practical print size merely to force a page fit;
- a portrait generated or added without the user's supplied image and choice.

## Score (100)

- hierarchy and first scan: 20
- typography and contrast: 15
- spacing and density: 15
- alignment and visual consistency: 15
- ATS reading order and text integrity: 15
- A4 page fit and print behavior: 15
- market and format fit: 5

Do not approve below 85/100 or with any blocking failure.

## Six-second visual scan

At normal scale, a reader should immediately identify the candidate, role
family, strongest proof, and current or most relevant experience. Decoration
must not compete with those signals.

## Template checks

- **Editorial**: accent edge is restrained; serif headings remain readable;
  optional portrait does not squeeze the identity block.
- **Minimal**: single reading order; no decoration required to understand the
  document; suitable for parser-sensitive upload.
- **Technical**: compact rhythm does not become dense noise; mono styling is
  limited to labels and metadata, never long body copy.
- **Compact density**: content was edited before spacing was reduced; no line
  height, page margin, or body size violates readability.

## Export checks

Inspect every page at 100%: top and bottom margins, heading orphans, bullet
splits, single-line page spills, portrait crop, link visibility, grayscale
contrast, and extracted text order. Record any check the current runtime could
not perform.
