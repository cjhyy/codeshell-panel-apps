# Resume focus rubric

Use this rubric for Base Resumes, JD Variants, revisions, and audits. Score only
what is visible in the public resume and supported by the evidence ledger.

## Blocking failures

Do not approve or save a polished draft when any of these is true:

- a public employer, date, ownership claim, technology, result, education item,
  or metric lacks a candidate Source;
- the target role or seniority is inflated beyond verified evidence;
- a JD phrase is presented as candidate experience without independent proof;
- more than six claims are marked core;
- the first scan is a technology catalog or biography rather than a hiring
  thesis;
- the same achievement appears in three or more sections;
- a core claim cannot produce a credible ownership or depth question.

## Scoring

| Dimension | Points | Full-credit test |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Positioning | 20 | One role family, plausible level, differentiated contribution, and proof are clear in the top third. |
| Evidence | 25 | Every material claim has a Source explanation; core claims have strong attribution and no invented facts. |
| Impact | 20 | Bullets describe changes, decisions, shipped behavior, or outcomes instead of responsibilities. |
| Relevance | 15 | The strongest role-family or JD signals lead; low-value detail is removed rather than merely moved. |
| Scanability | 10 | Hierarchy is obvious, bullets carry one idea, repetition is low, and the first scan works without evidence annotations. |
| Credibility | 10 | Language, level, metrics, scope, and chronology are precise enough to defend in an interview. |

Interpret the total:

- **90–100** — focused and application-ready after user fact review;
- **80–89** — strong draft with a small number of explicit improvements;
- **65–79** — usable source material, but revise before presenting as final;
- **below 65** — rebuild the focus brief and evidence selection first.

A high score never overrides a blocking failure.

## Six-second scan test

Read only the headline, professional summary, first capability line, and first
two bullets. Confirm that a recruiter can state:

- the candidate's role family;
- one difficult problem domain;
- one personally owned action or decision;
- one credible impact or concrete proof.

If two answers are missing, revise the top third before polishing later
sections.

## Signal-to-noise checks

- **Equal weight:** If every bullet sounds equally important, promote the best
  three signals and compress or remove the rest.
- **Technology soup:** If a bullet is mostly tools, move the tools behind the
  problem, decision, and impact.
- **Responsibility list:** If a bullet could describe any person in the role,
  require an owned action, constraint, or result.
- **Metric theater:** If a number lacks a source, baseline, or attribution,
  remove it and state the verified outcome.
- **JD echo:** If the resume mirrors requirements but evidence does not, mark a
  gap instead of claiming a match.
- **Project duplication:** If a work project repeats an experience bullet,
  keep it in the stronger location and use the second section for new proof.
- **Seniority by adjective:** Replace “senior,” “expert,” “excellent,” or
  “proficient” with scope, decisions, systems, stakeholders, or outcomes.

## Claim decision order

For every weak claim, choose the first honest action that works:

1. attach a stronger existing Source;
2. ask the user for a precise missing fact;
3. narrow or soften the wording;
4. demote it from core to supporting;
5. remove it from the public resume.

Never choose fabrication as the sixth option.

## Market and format notes

- Keep photo use optional and user-controlled. The Panel may support a photo,
  but the writing workflow must not add or infer one.
- Use the language requested by the user or established by the target market.
- Prefer simple headings and text hierarchy that survive PDF export and ATS
  extraction. Do not rely on icons, hidden keywords, or evidence annotations in
  the public document.
- Keep Base Resume content broader than one company while still making its role
  family and differentiation explicit.
